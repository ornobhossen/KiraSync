How it works

From sitting down to walking out, in eight steps.

01

Scan

Each table carries its own QR code. Scanning it identifies the restaurant, the table, and opens a live dining session automatically.

02

Add people

The first person names the group. Add, rename, or remove anyone at any point before the bill is settled.

03

Order

Browse the full menu together, in any order. Pick an item, and decide who it belongs to right away.

04

Assign food

Individual items go to one person. Shared items get split only between the people actually sharing them.

05

Share items

Select exactly who's sharing a dish — not the whole table by default — and watch the per-person cost divide instantly.

06

Review

See each person's bill broken down by item, and the table total, before anyone commits to paying.

07

Pay

Pay individually, or one person can settle the full outstanding balance — already-paid amounts are never charged twice.

08

Receipt

A digital receipt lands instantly, with the transaction ID, items, and amount — ready to show staff if ever needed.
